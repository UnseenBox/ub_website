# What Each of the 20 Bosses Does (Exactly)

Every fight = observe → learn → adapt → master. No shared template.

---

1. **Chairman — THE QUARTERLY REVIEW** (own script: `bossScripts/ChairmanScript.ts`). **Entrance:** the lights dip to a spotlight, he comes spinning through the door on the chair (untouchable), slides to a stop and bangs a gavel (`ORDER!`: a ring that shoves furniture and you, no damage), the photocopier is delivered, then the meeting opens with a QUICK REVIEW. Four telegraphed attacks on a shuffled deck: **QUICK REVIEW** (the chair spins up, a red lane with its wall bounce is drawn and locked, he drifts across the room; dizzy and +30% after), **REPLY ALL** (ring of paper planes, then a second ring turned to catch a player who stood still; a spinning 3-arm spiral in overtime), **Q3 PROJECTIONS** (overtime: a Q1/Q2/Q3 pie chart sweeps the floor, slices hurt, gaps are safe), **FORECLOSURE** (SOLD stamps with ticking rings on the things nearest you; unless you hold one when it runs out it is destroyed). Armour: 2 orbiting memos soak light hits, real hits tear them off, fire burns them all; last one gone = `OUT OF OFFICE!` (stunned, +35%) while the photocopier prints a new batch (jam it by hitting it, wreck it and he cannot reload). Counterplay: a heavy hit (>= 16) during a wind-up INTERRUPTS the move and stuns him; thrown objects SHRED planes; lure the drift through the copier ("RAM IT!" ring) and he crashes into it (-10% health, 2.6s stun, copier damaged). Below 25% health: FINAL QUARTER (back-to-back attacks, longer drifts, 5 stamps, tie on fire). Red glow and ear steam from overtime. He talks throughout. Death = `TERMINATED!` and a paper storm.
2. **Flusher — DO NOT STAND DOWNSTREAM** (own script: `bossScripts/FlusherScript.ts`). **Entrance:** a flood surges in through the door and he rides it into the room (untouchable, leaning into the wave), stops, and the lid slams: `FLUSH!`; the fight opens with geysers. **Armour:** the seat lid is down, so only 45% of hits get through (`CLANG!`). The lid lifts only when things go down the bowl. **Attacks (telegraphed, shuffled deck):** GEYSERS (red circles around you, one always under you, a ring shrinks, then water erupts), PLUNGER FAN (three aim lines, then plungers; a hit slows you 50% for 2s), TIDAL WAVE (a wall of water sweeps the room with one green safe gap; a second wave in overtime), WHIRLPOOL (a drain pulls you and the furniture toward his bowl; walk out or dash). **Counterplay:** anything pulled into the bowl is swallowed and hurts him whatever its size (`FLUSHED!`) and keeps the lid up for 4.5s; a heavy hit (>= 16) during a wind-up interrupts the move. Below 25% health: BURST PIPE (everything faster, more geysers, longer whirlpool, second tidal wave). He talks throughout. Death = `FLUSHED!`.
3. **Sofa Kraken — IT WANTS A HUG** (own script: `bossScripts/KrakenScript.ts`). A boss in parts. The sofa crawls in and settles in the middle of the room (the entrance: untouchable), then four tentacles burst up out of the floor one at a time. The sofa is COZY (only 10% of hits count) while any tentacle lives; each tentacle has its own health bar and only things you throw hurt it (touch aim assist aims at the tentacles). Cut them all down and the sofa is BARE (+40% damage, stars over his head, 5s) until they regrow. Tentacle attacks (telegraphed, one or two at a time): SLAM (a ring follows you, then locks and lands; the tentacle is then stuck and takes +50% from a hit), SWEEP (a pale fan shows the arc it will sweep round its root), GRAB (a dashed line shoots to you; if it lands you are held and squeezed every 0.7s: hit the tentacle to break free). Below 25% health: SOFA BED, six tentacles and everything faster. Taunts throughout. Death = `DEFLATED!`.
4. **Fridgezilla — SERVE CHILLED** (own script: `bossScripts/FridgeScript.ts`). **Entrance:** he skids in across the ice (untouchable), bangs to a stop and the door flies open (`MIDNIGHT SNACK!`); the fight opens with the cone. **Armour + positional play:** frost plates cover his front (two hits per plate, one heavy hit counts double) and only 10% of front hits get through; his compressor is on his BACK, marked on the floor with an orange `HIT HERE` wedge, and a hit from behind does 160% damage. His aim locks when an attack winds up, so the wind-up is your window to flank. Fire melts every plate (`MELTED!`). No plates = THAWED: stunned, dripping, +35% damage for 4.5s, then the frost regrows. **Attacks (telegraphed, shuffled deck):** DEEP FREEZE (a cone of cold, a second sweeping blast in overtime; hit = frozen to 30% speed for 1.6s), ICE CUBES (cubes that ricochet off the walls; thrown objects smash them), FISH LOB (frozen fish land on shrinking circles, one always under you, and leave you slipping), COLD SNAP (the floor turns icy except one green safe ring). Heavy hit (>= 16) during a wind-up interrupts. Below 25%: FREEZER BURN (pairs, much faster). Death = `DEFROSTED!`.
5. **Piano Spider — A FIGHT IN TIME** (own script: `bossScripts/PianoScript.ts`). Everything runs on a beat (100 BPM, 116 in overtime, 132 in the final phase) shown by a ring that closes on him every beat. **Rhythm combat:** a throw that lands ON the beat does x1.7 and builds a PERFECT streak (x2, x3 ... up to x2.5); off the beat it does x0.55; five perfect hits in a row is a CRESCENDO that stuns him. **Attacks (counted in beats, warned a bar ahead):** KEYBOARD (a giant keyboard is painted across the floor, some keys glow amber then flash red, then strike on the fourth beat: stand on the dark ones), STACCATO (he hops onto circles marked a beat earlier, three to five in a row), NOTES (a fan of musical notes every beat; thrown objects smash them), CRESCENDO (he crouches, then three rings of sound roll out a beat apart: dash through). **Entrance:** the lights drop, he is lowered on a thread (untouchable), crashes down, and counts you in (ONE TWO THREE FOUR). Below 25%: FORTISSIMO. Taunts throughout. Death = `FINALE!`.
6. **Mower Maniac — A FIGHT ABOUT FUEL** (own script: `bossScripts/MowerScript.ts`). A fuel gauge floats over him and drains all the time; every pass and spin costs more. At zero he STALLS (4.2s, +50% damage). To avoid that he drives to one of three JERRY CANS rolled into the corners (marked on the floor) and drinks it (refuels, heals 4%). Grab or destroy a can and he cannot use it; throw one at him and the explosion empties his tank on the spot (fire does too); with no cans left he stalls again and again. **Attacks (telegraphed, shuffled):** TRIPLE PASS (striped red lanes marked one at a time; he appears at the edge and charges the full length of the room; 3 lanes, 4 in overtime, 5 in the final phase), BLADE SPIN (a growing ring, then he spins while creeping toward you, dizzy afterwards), BLOWBACK (the leaf blower: a cone that shoves you and the furniture away). **Entrance:** a roar, a long drift in through the door, a rev (`VROOOM!`), cans rolled in; the fight opens with a pass. Below 25%: FULL THROTTLE (faster, more lanes, tank drains faster). Taunts throughout. Death = `RETIRED!`.
7. **Mom — HOUSE RULES** (own script: `bossScripts/MomScript.ts`). A boss who makes rules. Every so often she plants her feet and holds up a sign with a countdown: NO RUNNING! (stand still), CORNER. NOW. (be inside the glowing corner when the timer ends), NO THROWING! (throw nothing), TIDY UP! (pick something up). Obey and she sighs `...FINE.`: her guard drops and she takes +50% for 4.5s. Break the rule and you are hurt, she heals 2.5% and says `I TOLD YOU!`. **Her guard:** the rest of the time anything you throw at her is CAUGHT and CONFISCATED (the object is destroyed, 10% damage lands); fire and explosions get through, and she is distracted (+20%) while she shouts a rule or throws slippers. Between rules she throws SLIPPERS: three aim lines, then a fan of slippers, two or three volleys. **Entrance:** the room is dark, she stands in the doorway, the lights CLICK on, she marches in and demands `WHO DID THIS?!`, then the first rule. Below 25%: GROUNDED (rules and slippers back to back, shorter timers). Taunts throughout. Death = `BEDTIME!`.
8. **Vacuumasaurus — A FIGHT ON A LEAD** (own script: `bossScripts/VacuumScript.ts`). A power cord runs from a wall socket to his back (drawn on the floor, with a pulsing `PULL THE PLUG` target). While PLUGGED IN only 60% of hits count; a thrown object through the socket UNPLUGS him: no suction and no attacks, +40% damage, and he drags himself back to the wall at a crawl and plugs in again (then a 7s cooldown before it can be pulled again). **Attacks (telegraphed, shuffled):** INHALE (a cone, or 360 degrees in the final phase, pulls you and the room toward his mouth; small things that reach it are SWALLOWED into the DUST BAG, shown over him; the mouth hurts), SPIT (aim lines, then everything in the bag, at least three things, is fired at you in a fan and hurts whatever it is), CORD WHIP (a fan shows the arc, then the lead sweeps round him; two whips in the final phase). **Entrance:** the lights flicker, he rolls in unspooling the cord, a whine (`VRRRRRRR!`), then the first inhale. Below 25%: FULL SUCTION. Taunts throughout. Death = `EXTINCT!`.
9. **Laundry Hydra — CUT A HEAD, TWO GROW BACK** (own script: `bossScripts/HydraScript.ts`). A washing machine with three sock-puppet heads on long necks (each with a health bar). The machine is SOAKED: only 10% of hits count while any head is alive. Thrown things knock heads down, but a head cut down SPLITS into two smaller ones (up to six heads) unless the stump is BURNED: a head hit by a hot iron (two are rolled into the corners, ringed on the floor) or anything on fire is CAUTERIZED and stays gone. With every head gone for good the machine is HEADLESS (+50% damage). Touch aim assist aims at the heads. **Head attacks (telegraphed, up to three at once as the head count grows):** BITE (a ring marks the landing, it bites, then is stuck: +50% to a hit) and SWEEP (a fan shows the arc round the machine). **Entrance:** the washer shudders to the middle of the room, DING!, and the heads burst out one by one; banner `CUT A HEAD, TWO GROW BACK / burn the stump`. Taunts throughout. Death = `FOLDED!`.
10. **Fan-tastic** — REBUILT (bossScripts/FanScript.ts). A fight in the wind: a swinging wind (floor chevrons + compass over his head, periodic GUSTs) shoves you, the furniture and bends every throw. He hovers (hits graze for 20%); the way to hurt him is to bait a SWOOP (red lane telegraph, 2–4 dives) because after the last dive he CRASH-LANDS and is GROUNDED for 3.6s at +60% damage. TORNADO (marked spawns, drift toward you and drag you in) and CYCLONE (expanding rings to dash through). Final phase TURBO at 25% HP: stronger wind, more dives and twisters. Entrance: the room goes still, he drops from the ceiling spinning up, the wind rises, then he opens with a swoop. Death = switched off, ...whirr... click.

11. **Popcorn** — REBUILT (bossScripts/PopcornScript.ts). A fight about hot potatoes: he lobs fused KERNEL BOMBS (fuse ring + burst radius drawn); a burst hurts whoever is inside, so pick one up and throw it back: a bomb bursting on him takes 12% max HP, while ordinary hits are CHIPped to 35% (greasy). LOB (he pops across the room, marked ring, 1–3 bombs), SPRAY (drawn cone, 2–4 volleys), OVERFLOW (growing circle, blast, then he is an EMPTY TUB for 3.4s at +50% and the overflow leaves bombs to throw). Final phase EXTRA BUTTER at 25%: shorter fuses, more bombs. Entrance: kernels pop one by one into the tub, faster and faster, eyes open, one slow bomb. Death = POPPED HIS LAST.

12. **Cable Kraken** — ZAP + LASH. Slow crawl, creates electric zone (`zaps`) that shocks player + other enemies. Lashes in cone. Must stay out of electric radius + dodge lash. Death = cable burns, final zap

13. **Pressure Cooker** — STEAM + SLAM. Slow crawl, slam creates steam zone (`spawnZone: steam`) that hurts + makes floor slippery. 4th phase at 0.15 (bigger shockwave). Must avoid steam + dodge slam. Death = steam release, `DEPRESSURIZED!`

14. **Midnight Express** — TRACK RUN. Picks lane, whistles (`alarm`), very long lunge (500 range, 800 speed, 0.9s). Must NOT be on track. Death = derails, smoke

15. **King of Junk** — ORBIT + WEEKLY HURL. Slow crawl, pulls furniture into orbit (`pull: 500`), then throws back (`plank`). Must dodge orbit + thrown pieces. Death = tower collapses, `JUNK REMOVED!`

16. **The House** — WALL THROW + SLAM. Very slow crawl, throws pieces of itself (`plank` hurl, range 500), slams (radius 160). Must stay away + dodge thrown planks. 4th phase at 0.15. Death = building collapses, `CONDemned`

17. **Time-Thief** — TIME RING. Slow crawl, creates time-freeze bubble (`stasis`: slow player to 0.25, reverse furniture velocity). Throws alarm clock. Must move before ring hits + dodge hurl. Death = clock stops, sparks

18. **Toybox Titan** — SPAWN ESCALATION. Slow crawl, summons toys (`gnat` → `mini` → `nested`) every 8s (escalates at 3rd spawn). Must kill toys + dodge slam. Death = crank breaks, stars

19. **Dishwasher Demon** — FLOOD + PLATE ROLL. Slow crawl, creates water puddles (`seeps`), throws plates down wet floor (rolls, slippery). Must not chase through water + dodge rolling plates. Death = drains, splash sound

20. **King of Cardboard** — 3 FORMS (tank/chopper/robot with red/yellow/blue ring). Crawls → swoops (1.7x speed, 320 lunge) → throws. Announces `TANK!`/`CHOPPER!`/`ROBOT!`. Must react instantly to form change. 4th phase at 0.15 (nested + shockwave). Death = all 3 collapse, `FLAT-PACKED!`
