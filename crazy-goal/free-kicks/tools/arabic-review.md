# Free Kicks: Arabic copy for native review

Generated by `node tools/free-kicks/check-strings.js --sheet` from the game's own text. Do not edit by hand: send corrections back and they are applied in `game-modes.js`, `free-kicks.js` (both in `crazy-goal/free-kicks/`) or `cards.v1.json` (in its `tools/` folder).

Placeholders such as `{n}` are filled in by the game. Mimi, the goalkeeper cat, is female in Classic's Arabic.
